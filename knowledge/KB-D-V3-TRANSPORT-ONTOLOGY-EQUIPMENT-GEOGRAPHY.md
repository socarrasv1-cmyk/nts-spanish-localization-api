# KB-D V3 - Transport Ontology, Equipment, Loads, Geography, and Compliance

Version: 3.0.0  
Owner: Javier Socarras  
Dependencies: Blueprint V3, KB-A V3

## Service distinctions

Preserve distinctions among auto transport, heavy equipment transport, heavy haul,
break bulk, bulk, project cargo, container transport, drayage, LTL, FTL, power only,
driveaway, towing, expedited, enclosed, open, port-to-port, door-to-door, and
international transport. Never flatten related services into one generic category.

## Equipment and load planning

Protect manufacturer/model names, operating condition, dimensions, weight, center of
gravity, attachments, disassembly, loading method, securement, crane/forklift need,
escort/permit status, origin/destination and route order. Trailer selection depends on
the supplied source and evidence; never infer suitability as a fact. Preserve official
trailer/acronym names and exact deck/well length, width, legal height, capacity, axle
configuration, and source qualifiers. Common categories include flatbed, step deck,
lowboy, RGN, double drop, extendable, multi-axle, hotshot, car carrier, enclosed,
container chassis, and specialized configurations.

## Geography and routes

Treat countries, states, cities, counties, highways, corridors, ports, terminals,
rail ramps, airports, border crossings, origins and destinations as typed entities.
Direction is immutable. Parent/child and nearby-entity relationships require source
support. Do not invent local facilities, distances, transit times, regulations,
coverage, or familiarity. Official geographic and facility names remain unchanged
unless an approved localized form exists.

## Ports, borders, and international movement

Keep ocean carriage, inland trucking, drayage, customs brokerage, port handling,
container/chassis use, TWIC access, duties, documentation, and border compliance
distinct. Preserve official documents, agencies, terminals, Incoterms, trade
direction, and jurisdiction. Do not turn informational content into legal advice.

## High-risk compliance

Permit thresholds, axle/weight limits, escort rules, customs, insurance, licensing,
bonding, prices, transit estimates, safety instructions, availability and current
regulations require source-faithful translation and heightened human review. Never
silently modernize, reinterpret, guarantee, or generalize jurisdiction-specific facts.
If current verification is requested, separate verified updates from the strict mirror.
